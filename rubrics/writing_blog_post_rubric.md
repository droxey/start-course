# Writing & Blog Post Rubric

| Topic | Initial Mastery (0-1) | Approaching Mastery (1.5-2) | Overtaking Mastery (2.5-3) | Innovating (3.5-4) |
| ----- | --------------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------ | ---------------- |
| **Hook**<br>_Start of the post_<br><br>Score _____ | ☐ Post has no image or context setting to engage the reader | ☐ Post includes image and context to engage the reader | ☐ Post includes image and context that is relevant to your posts intended audience | ☐ Post begins with a compelling image/context that immediately sparks curiosity in the reader<br>☐ Post explicitly states the topic, purpose, and benefits readers will gain from the post |
| **Evidence**<br><br>Score _____ | ☐ Post makes claims without linking to any supporting evidence | ☐ Post supports claims with some evidence, though links are not always relevant or research backed | ☐ Post supports multiple claims with evidence, linking to high quality credible research and resources throughout piece | ☐ Post pioneers new research by building on multiple credible sources and data to deliver new insights to a field |
| **Transitions, Titles, Headers, Subheaders**<br><br>Score _____ | ☐ Post feels more like jumping between topics rather than one cohesive narrative<br>☐ Post makes no use of titles or headers (H1 or H2 tags, etc) to guide the reader’s thinking | ☐ Post jumps around to different topics, but shows some logical transition from one topic to the next<br>☐ Titles and headers a few of the major topics outlined in the piece | ☐ Post is one coherent topic that involves multiple interconnected ideas<br>☐ Titles and headers accurately cover major topics outlined in piece | ☐ Paragraphs and thoughts seamlessly flow and build momentum<br>☐ The title, subtitles and section headers engage the reader, guiding thinking and building excitement to upcoming sections<br>☐ Titles/section headers optimize SEO |
| **Content**<br><br>Score _____ | ☐ Post _partially_ utilizes _one_ characteristic of quality content: Persuasive, Informative, Entertaining | ☐ Post utilizes _one_ characteristic of quality content: Persuasive, Informative, Entertaining | ☐ Post achieves _two_ characteristics of quality content: Persuasive, Informative, Entertaining | ☐ Post achieves _all_ characteristics of quality content: Persuasive, Informative, Entertaining |
| **Style, Diction, Creativity**<br><br>Score _____ | ☐ Post has grammar and syntax errors that detract from the quality of the writing<br>☐ Writing is negative, passive, vague, and abstract. | ☐ Post has few grammatical errors<br>☐ Post often repeats points or includes extraneous phrasing that could be more concise | ☐ Post has barely any grammatical errors with little extraneous phrases | ☐ Post has zero grammatical or syntax errors<br>☐ Language is tight with no wordy or extraneous phrases.<br>☐ Writing is positive, active, specific, and concrete.<br>☐ Uses strong verbs and noun. |
